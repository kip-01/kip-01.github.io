TerraOre: community and environmental compliance

As CSR, Environmental and Community Relations Coordinator, I led community engagement for a minerals leaching plant from first contact to start-up, and supported its environmental licence.

TerraOre
7households relocated and compensated
1NEMA environmental impact assessment licence supported
1Ministry of Mining registration

The challenge

A processing plant changes life for the households and groups around it. The work was to make that change fair, documented and compliant, while keeping local leaders and government offices informed.

What I did

  • Mapped affected households, local leaders and community groups, and led engagement from first contact to start-up.
  • Built the phased relocation appraisal for seven affected households and coordinated their compensation through cash payments and new houses on purchased, titled land.
  • Supported the NEMA Environmental Impact Assessment for the Molowa plant and followed up its licence conditions.
  • Registered the company with the Ministry of Mining.
  • Represented the Ikolomani Youth Cooperative at a Ministry of Mining stakeholders' workshop chaired by the Principal Secretary in January 2025.
  • Reported progress to company management and kept working relationships open with host community members and government offices.

Why it matters

This is the same practice I used on peace and livelihoods work: understand who is affected, give them a real voice, and keep the paperwork honest. I am a NEMA-licensed Associate Expert (EIA-MP/153/2018).
