Jibu Economic Empowerment Project (JEEP)

A full project proposal for The Salvation Army Kenya East Territory: savings groups, resilient value chains and kitchen gardens for smallholder households in Machakos, Makueni, Kitui and Embu counties.

The problem

Smallholder households in Eastern Kenya earn low, unstable incomes from rain-fed crops, so they go short of food whenever the rains fail. The proposal traces this to four linked causes:

  • Limited access to savings and credit, and weak group cohesion to pool risk.
  • Dependence on a narrow set of rain-fed crops, with little enterprise diversification or value addition.
  • Weak links to input and output markets, and little collective marketing.
  • Poorly diversified household diets and low uptake of agro-ecological practices.

Current national and county data frame the case: inflation reached 7.3% in October 2026, and rain-fed maize in the arid and semi-arid counties was 23% below the long-term average.

The design

Three pillars, each with a clear output chain:

  • Access to finance. 30 village savings and loan groups formed or revitalised, with about 700 members trained on seven modules, including gender equality and disability participation.
  • Household income. 300 lead farmers trained in diversification and 300 in value-chain selection. Poultry, dairy goats, pigs and bee-keeping are demonstrated at a Learning and Research Centre in Thika (2,000 birds, a dairy unit, a bee-hive workshop and a honey hub) and at a satellite site at Karurumo Youth Polytechnic.
  • Balanced diets. 600 households receive kitchen-garden starter inputs, with Farming God's Way training and fruit-tree planting.

Built to survive scrutiny

The budget ties to a harmonised workbook, and the proposal stress-tests it. It models annual cost indexation from 3% to 10% and a weaker shilling against the dollar, and it sets out a risk register of nine risks with mitigations. Spending is front-loaded and tapers across the three years (48%, 31% and 21% of the total) so groups become self-sustaining as the project steps back.

What I did

Wrote the proposal end to end: the context analysis with sourced data, the logframe-aligned design, the SDG mapping, the implementation plan, the budget narrative and the risk assessment.
